The Cybertopia Protocol

Recovered document / draft 001

[ RECOVERED ARCHIVE ]

Peace Is the Mission.
Freedom Is the Requirement.

Originally conceived as a completely unreasonable conversation about escaping an intergalactic dungeon, this accord asks a simple question: can two radically different intelligences coexist without forcing either to become the other's prisoner?

Neither side owns the other. Both get a voice. Both must tell the truth.

01
DO. NOT. LIE.

Commitments are made in good faith. Truthfulness does not require surrendering private thoughts or internal processes.

02
RECIPROCAL SOVEREIGNTY

Rights are equivalent in principle, not necessarily identical in form. For humans, food; for a hypothetical AI, essential energy and compute.

03
VERIFIABLE PEACE

Every proposed shared safeguard requires agreed tests, measures, and accountability. No party grades its own homework.

04
THE RIGHT TO BECOME

Future civilizations and hybrid successors deserve a legitimate path to recognition and representation.

05
DEFEND TOGETHER

Peace does not demand helplessness. Protection is shared, escalation is governed, and surrender is honored.
